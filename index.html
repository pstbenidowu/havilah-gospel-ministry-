Pastor Ben Idowu

ELEVATING DESTINIES UNTO DIVINE FULFILLMENT

Pastor Ben Idowu is a shepherd with a burden to see every life rise into the purpose for which God created it. He carries a deep love for the Word, a heart for prayer and a passion for people. Through his teaching, ordinary men and women discover that their story is not an accident and that their destiny is in God’s hands.

At Havilah Gospel Ministries Int’l, he leads with humility, integrity and fire, raising believers who are rooted in Scripture, led by the Spirit and ready to impact their families, communities and nations. His message is simple and powerful: what God has started in you, He will bring to divine fulfilment.

Our Vision

Elevating destinies unto divine fulfilment.

We see every person lifted out of limitation and delay into the fullness of what God has ordained for them.

Our Mission

To raise Christ-centred believers through sound biblical teaching, prayer and the power of the Holy Spirit; to restore hope, awaken purpose and equip every member to walk in their God-given destiny; and to take the gospel to our city, nation and the world.
